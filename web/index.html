        Nova campus · unofficial student handbook

        How to play

        It is exam season on campus. Most of you are students with paperwork to do. A few of you are examiners in disguise, and they did not come to help.

        Background music

        The two roles

        • Student — survive the semester. Finish your to-do list and find out who the examiners are.
        • Undercover Examiner — fail them all. Blend in, hand out failed exams and do not get caught.

        Controls

        • WASD or the arrow keys: walk
        • F use a task place or fix the lights
        • R report a failed exam · M call a meeting at the table
        • E expel · V vent · Q lights off (examiners only)
        • H this handbook · Esc close it
        • C campus map, to say where you were
        • Drag the joystick (bottom left) to walk.
        • Tap the round buttons (bottom right) to act. A grey button tells you why it is not possible right now.
        • The map button (top right) shows the whole campus, to say where you were.

        Tasks

        Your to-do list names three errands. Walk to the glowing object, press Use and solve the little job. Every finished task fills the bar at the top. Ghosts can still finish theirs.

        Expelling

        An examiner next to a student hands over a failed exam (6/20). The student is expelled, becomes a ghost and the exam stays on the floor. Then the examiner has to wait 20 seconds.

        Reporting and meetings

        Found a failed exam? Report it. Or press the red button on the meeting table, once per round. Everyone then meets, talks and votes: the player with the most votes is sent surfing. A tie or a skip majority sends nobody.

        Vents

        Examiners can climb into a floor vent, travel unseen to another vent with the arrows and climb out there. Others only see the hatch move, never who is inside.

        Sabotage

        Examiners can switch the lights off. Students then hardly see anything until one of them stands at the light switch for 3 seconds.

        How each side wins

        • Students win when all tasks are done or every examiner is voted out.
        • Examiners win when there are as many examiners left as students.
